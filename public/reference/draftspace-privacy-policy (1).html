DraftSpace

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how DraftSpace (the "Service," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you use our website and services.

DraftSpace is a student writing-feedback platform designed to help students improve their writing through personalized human feedback. This policy applies to information collected through the DraftSpace website, including information submitted when creating an account and using the Service.

2. Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account using Google Sign-In, we receive information associated with your Google account, including your name and email address. We also process account identifiers and authentication information necessary to create, maintain, and secure your DraftSpace account.

Writing submissions. When you submit writing through DraftSpace, we collect and store the contents of your submission, including your essay or draft, its title, and the type of writing you identify it as. Your submissions may contain personal information that you choose to include in your writing.

Writing preferences and profile information. We collect information you provide about your writing preferences and other information associated with your account. This information helps us understand your goals and provide more relevant feedback.

Feedback and annotations. When feedback is provided on your writing, we store the associated comments, annotations, highlights, and other feedback information. This may include strengths, areas for improvement, observations about your writing voice, and suggested next steps.

Messages and notifications. When you communicate through DraftSpace, we collect and store the messages you send and receive through the platform. We may also store notification information, including notification content and whether a notification has been read.

Technical information. We may collect technical information necessary to operate and secure the Service, including authentication records, account activity, timestamps, browser or device information, and operational logs generated by our infrastructure and service providers.

Cookies and local storage. DraftSpace may store information in your browser, including information needed to maintain your session and remember certain preferences or settings. Additional details are provided in Section 4.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • create, maintain, and authenticate DraftSpace accounts;
  • receive, store, and organize writing submissions;
  • provide personalized human feedback on submitted writing;
  • display feedback, comments, and annotations associated with submissions;
  • maintain conversations and notifications within the platform;
  • remember user preferences and improve the experience of using DraftSpace;
  • troubleshoot technical problems and maintain the security of the Service;
  • respond to questions, requests, and reports of misuse; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations.

DraftSpace is designed to provide feedback through human review. We do not intend to use submitted writing to generate automated AI-written feedback for students.

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not make decisions about users that produce legal or similarly significant effects through automated profiling.

4. Cookies and local storage

DraftSpace may use browser storage technologies, including local storage and cookies, to maintain account sessions, remember preferences, and support essential website functionality.

These technologies may store information such as authentication-related data, onboarding preferences, and other settings necessary for the Service to function.

We may also use analytics technologies to understand how visitors interact with DraftSpace and to improve the website. Any analytics services in use may collect technical information about website visits and browser activity.

You can manage or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. However, removing certain stored information may affect your ability to remain signed in or use some features.

5. How information is disclosed

Who can access your information. Your account information, writing submissions, and associated feedback are not publicly accessible to other students. Access to student submissions is restricted to the DraftSpace administrator, who may review them to provide feedback, maintain the Service, respond to support requests, and address technical or security issues.

Service providers. DraftSpace uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate its services. These providers may process or store information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services. This includes:

  • Google. Google provides the authentication service used when you sign in with your Google account. Google's handling of information is also governed by its own privacy policy.
  • Firebase. Firebase supports account authentication and related account functionality.
  • Supabase. Supabase provides database and backend infrastructure used to store and manage information associated with DraftSpace accounts, submissions, feedback, and platform functionality.

We do not authorize service providers to use student submissions for their own unrelated purposes.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of DraftSpace, its users, or others.

Transfers. If DraftSpace or its operations are transferred to another operator, information may be transferred as part of that transition, subject to applicable privacy obligations.

6. Data retention

We retain account information and associated platform information for as long as necessary to operate the Service and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Writing submissions, feedback, messages, and other information associated with an account may remain stored while the account is active.

If you would like your account or personal information deleted, you may contact us using the email address provided in Section 13. Deletion requests are handled manually. We will review and respond to requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Some information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or technical records after a deletion request has been processed.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access: Request information about the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Withdraw consent: Where processing is based on your consent, withdraw that consent where applicable.
  • Other applicable rights: Exercise additional rights provided under the privacy laws applicable to your location.

To make a request or ask a question about your personal information, contact us using the information in Section 13.

We will not discriminate against users for exercising applicable privacy rights.

8. Children

DraftSpace is intended for individuals aged 13 and older. Individuals under the age of 13 are not permitted to create or use a DraftSpace account.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that an account belonging to a child under 13 has been created or that we have collected their personal information, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation, including deleting the information where required.

A parent or guardian who believes a child under 13 has provided personal information to DraftSpace may contact us using the information in Section 13.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored and processed through DraftSpace. These measures include restricting access to student submissions and using authentication and access controls to protect accounts and platform data.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored through the Service.

10. International users

DraftSpace uses third-party infrastructure providers to operate the Service. As a result, information may be processed or stored in the United States or other countries where these providers maintain their services.

Data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws in your location.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to DraftSpace, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Where appropriate, we may also provide notice of significant changes through the Service.

12. Platform feedback and testimonials

From time to time, DraftSpace may invite users to share optional feedback about the platform. This feedback may include:

  • a star rating out of five;
  • written comments about your experience;
  • suggestions for new features; and
  • a preference indicating that you do not want to receive future feedback requests.

Feedback you submit is stored in the DraftSpace database and may be associated with your account. You may close a feedback prompt without responding or choose not to receive future requests. Participation is optional and does not affect your access to DraftSpace.

Public display is separate and optional. Selected ratings or comments may appear on the DraftSpace homepage as testimonials, but only if you separately opt in to public display. Submitting feedback does not automatically give us permission to publish it. You can submit feedback without agreeing to public display.

If a testimonial is published:

  • it will be displayed in de-identified form;
  • your name, email address, and account identifier will not be displayed alongside it; and
  • you can request its removal at any time by contacting draftspacesupport@gmail.com.

Please avoid including identifying information, such as your name, school, or location, in any feedback you submit.
